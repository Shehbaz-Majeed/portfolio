The brief

Specialist retail is not just about showing more products. Customers often need to narrow a catalog, understand fit, and find the practical information that removes doubt.

For Kiln Frog, the work centred on collection browsing and support content: make filtering more usable, put helpful answers where they are needed, and keep the experience responsive.

What I built

  1. 01

    Collection filtering behaviour

    Theme-level controls and interface states that make it easier to move through product categories and filter a larger technical catalog.

  2. 02

    Helpful content patterns

    Expandable FAQs and reusable support-oriented blocks that let the storefront answer common questions without taking customers away from shopping.

  3. 03

    Responsive theme improvements

    Focused front-end refinements across navigation, product cards, and collection layouts to keep product information clear on smaller screens.

Implementation note

When a product catalog is technical, discovery and reassurance are part of the same job. The best filtering UI still needs the supporting information nearby.
