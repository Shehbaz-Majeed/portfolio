The brief

Farm-box customers need to understand what they are getting, when it arrives, and how the recurring order fits their routine before they commit.

The work focused on making those decisions legible in the storefront—keeping seasonal context, box choices, and subscription mechanics close to the point of purchase.

What I built

  1. 01

    Subscription-aware product experience

    Product-page patterns that make one-time and recurring options easier to compare before a shopper adds a box to cart.

  2. 02

    Flexible seasonal content

    Theme sections that support changing produce stories, box details, and campaign messaging without requiring a new page build every time.

  3. 03

    Practical cart behaviour

    Cart and ordering details designed around the questions that matter most for recurring delivery: selection, timing, and the next step.

Implementation note

For subscriptions, the right interface reduces uncertainty first. Price, frequency, delivery expectations, and the selected option should never be competing for attention.
