The brief

The storefront needed to carry the confidence of a beauty brand while keeping the shopping path clear across a content-rich catalog.

I approached the work as a theme system rather than a single page: build flexible sections for campaigns and merchandising, then keep the product journey focused and responsive.

What I built

  1. 01

    Reusable campaign sections

    Configurable Liquid sections and blocks for editorial imagery, launch messaging, product callouts, and branded content—managed through the Shopify theme editor.

  2. 02

    Product-led page patterns

    Clear templates and components that help shoppers move from visual inspiration to product details, variants, and purchase decisions.

  3. 03

    Responsive visual refinement

    Layout, typography, spacing, and interaction adjustments so the experience retains its character without becoming hard to use on small screens.

Implementation note

Content-heavy storefronts work best when the theme editor controls map to a small set of reliable visual patterns—not a separate custom layout for every campaign.
