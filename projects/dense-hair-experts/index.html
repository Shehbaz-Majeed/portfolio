The brief

The product page needed to support three ways to buy: a one-time purchase, a one-month plan with a 20% saving, and a three-month plan with a 30% saving.

Rather than relying on a generic subscription widget, I shaped the interface around the actual product decision and connected the selected plan to the correct selling plan and cart quantity.

What I built

  1. 01

    Custom plan selector

    A tailored storefront interface for one-time, one-month, and three-month purchase options with live plan-aware price presentation.

  2. 02

    Selling-plan integration

    Selected options are passed to the product form through Shopify selling-plan data, keeping the standard add-to-cart flow compatible with the custom UI.

  3. 03

    Quantity rule for the three-month plan

    The three-month option updates the line-item quantity to match the fulfilment approach, while the rest of the product form remains familiar to the shopper.

Implementation note

Subscription UI should represent the merchant’s actual delivery model. The key is not adding more controls—it is keeping plan, discount, frequency, and cart data in agreement.
