What I help with

From an initial brief to the details that make a theme easier to use and maintain.

  1. 01

    Theme development

    Custom Shopify sections, templates, blocks, and schema built to give teams practical control after handoff.

  2. 02

    Product & cart experiences

    Variant logic, product options, subscription choices, AJAX cart behaviour, and interface states that make purchasing feel straightforward.

  3. 03

    Storefront refinement

    Responsive fixes, UX improvements, performance-minded cleanup, and thoughtful implementation of a Figma or existing brand direction.

How I work

A practical process, with the storefront and the customer both in view.

01

Clarify the job

We define the page purpose, customer decisions, theme constraints, and what success should look like before the build starts.

02

Build the right system

I translate the brief into reusable theme patterns—not a pile of one-off fixes that become hard to manage later.

03

Check the experience

Before handoff, I test the important paths across devices and make sure the interface behaves as clearly as it looks.
